Help

Questions, answered.

Can't find it here? Our assistant is in the corner of the page, any time.

Are your clinicians registered?

Yes. Every clinician is registered with the relevant UK professional body and vetted before joining. You can see their credentials on their profile.

How quickly can I be seen?

Most GP appointments are available the same day, often within minutes. Specialist referrals are usually arranged within 48 hours.

Can I use this for an emergency?

No. If it's a medical emergency, call 999 or go to your nearest A&E. For urgent but non-emergency advice, call NHS 111.

Is my health data private?

Your record is encrypted and only shared with your explicit permission. You can download or delete your data at any time.

Can I book for my family?

Yes — add family members to your account and manage appointments, prescriptions and results for everyone in one dashboard.
